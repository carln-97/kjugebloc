Hörnet is Kjugekull’s most problem-rich sector, tucked into the southeastern corner of the forest, bordering a quiet apple orchard. The sector is shaded by a mix of beech and towering pine trees, creating a lush canopy that provides cool summer shade and and some shelter from light rain.

In its center lies a massive chunk of granite, shattered by time into a chaotic pile of boulders and surreal rock-formations. Here you'll find some of Kjugekull’s most dramatic and imposing highballs. These lines are bold, beautiful, and demand full commitment along with a strong head. Surrounding these giants is a generous spread of shorter, less committing problems. The variety is impressive – you’ll encounter everything from delicate slabs to burly mantles and overhangs. Despite the area's popularity, there’s still space to explore and plenty of hidden gems to discover.
