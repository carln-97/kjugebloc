Vånga is a large area with lots of scattered blocs, some are just roadside others hidden deeper in the woods. Although many boulders only host one or a few lines – it's definitely quality over quantity here. The rock is a bit on the rougher side but with plenty of proud and inspiring lines, requiring a few pads. Most of the landings however are quite good and there're also sectors with not so tall climbs of great quality and movement. Climbing styles vary a lot in Vånga with everything from towering intricate slabs and technical crack-lines to small powerful overhangs and boardlike walls! The approaches are generally not very far, even though some sectors have a remote and wild feeling to them, located far from houses and people.

!!<s>ACCESS:</s> many of the blocs are located on narrow forest roads with limited parking spot – always make sure large vehicles can pass by.!!
