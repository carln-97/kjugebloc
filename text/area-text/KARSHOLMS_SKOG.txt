Karsholms skog is medium sized area consisting of several smaller varying sectors with separate parkings. The original sector is 'U Boat' located in an open and wild forest with smaller rocks of good quality sporting flat landings. Here the climbing is powerful on fairly good holds with somewhat longer approaches.

'Sohallen' comes next: a large freestanding boulder with some airy lines requiring big moves on positive holds. This bloc offers plenty of hard to very hard projects all in an unusually powerful style! The downsides are rougher rock-quality as well as a very long approach.

Some other smaller blocs are also tucked away in the surrounding landscape and may be worth exploring for the curious.
