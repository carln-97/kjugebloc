Utsidan is the first sight meeting you when approaching Kjugekull from the parking lot via the café. Situated just outside the fence that encloses the main part of the forest, it spans the entire outside, making it one of the larger sectors. <b>Grinden</b> and <b>Caféblocket</b> are the open parts of Utsidan, while the rest of the boulders are nestled within spacious pine forest. Most of the blocs are of moderate size, with a few larger exceptions scattered among the trees. Landings range from smooth and flat in the open parts to more uneven and rocky in the forested sections.

Blocs near the café and parking area are especially popular, while many others—though easily accessed via the main trail—remain quieter and more peaceful. One notable exception is the iconic Nä, men Jeppe, among the most renowned easy problems at Kjugekull.
