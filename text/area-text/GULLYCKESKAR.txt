Gullyckeskär is a peaceful area on the forest’s edge, where the remnants of an old quarry add to the ambiance with massive rock heaps rising in the background. The main sector <b>Kungasalen</b> or the King's Hall has gotten it's name from the Swedish king Oscar I's three sons whom upon visiting the area in 1846 engraved their names in largest boulder.

All rocks are of the characteristic reddish and rough Vånga-granite, with blocs of varying sizes from lowballs to some taller striking lines! The approach to the main sector is short and easy, however for the splendid <b>Dragahall-bloc</b> one has a ways to go up the hill – though the central line "Solitaire" is certainly worth the effort!

!!<s>ACCESS:</s> the sector <s>'Kungasalen'</s> has some engravings on the central bloc, do not climb on or damage these. The sector <s>'Johans sva'</s> lies in a paddock close to some houses, please show extra discretion here.!!
