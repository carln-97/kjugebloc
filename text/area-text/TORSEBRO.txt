Torsebro is the second most classical bouldering spot in Skåne after Kjugekull. The blocs are scarcely dispersed along a small gravel road in an open beech forest with mixed in patches of firs. Most of the rocks are fairly large and a few are huge, however pretty much all of them have good landings. Regarding the rock quality, it's about the same as Kjugekull though a handful of blocs have more fine grained rock with less crystals. There are also a good few steep climbs with powerful moves, which you rarely find in these parts. With an easy approach, lots of climbs in most styles and all grades; Torsebro is a great area for beginners as well as experienced climbers!

!!ACCESS: the sectors 'Projektstenen', 'Tres Mosqueteros' and 'Ebbetorpsstenen' are close to residential houses – be respectful and keep an extra low profile. Make sure to access 'Projektstenen' and 'Tres Mosqueteros' by walking around the houses in the forest on the left.!!
