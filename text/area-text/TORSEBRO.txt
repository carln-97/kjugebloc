Torsebro was the second climbing area to be explored back in the day after Kjugekull. The blocs are scarcely dispersed along a small gravel road in an open beech forest with mixed in patches of firs. Most of the blocs are fairly large and a few are positively huge, however more or less all landings are good. The rock quality is similar to that of Kjugekull though a handful of blocs have more fine grained rock with less crystals. There are also a good few steep climbs with powerful moves, which you rarely find in these parts. The easy approach, volume of climbs in most styles and wide span of grades makes Torsebro a great area for beginners as well as experienced climbers!

!!<s>ACCESS:</s> the sectors <s>'Projektstenen', 'Tres Mosqueteros'</s> and <s>'Ebbetorpsstenen'</s> are close to residential houses – be respectful and show extra discretion here. Make sure to access <s>'Projektstenen'</s> and <s>'Tres Mosqueteros'</s> by walking around the houses in the forest on the left.!!
