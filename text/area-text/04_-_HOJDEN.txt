At the heart of Kjugekull, a small hill rises out of the meadows, its slopes dotted with juniper bushes and clusters of boulders. Dispersed across both open ground and light forest, the blocs here offer mostly easy to moderate climbs above green landings.

Most lines are of medium height on freestanding boulders, with varied shapes and orientations. In the background, a few taller formations rise up offering more committing climbs for those seeking height and exposure.
