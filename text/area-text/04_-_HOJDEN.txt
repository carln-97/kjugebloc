At the center of Kjugekull, a gentle hill rises from the surrounding meadows. Its slopes are scattered with boulders, and during spring dotted with juniper bushes. The blocs, spread across open clearings and light forest, offer primarily easy to moderate climbs with grassy landings.

Most problems are of medium height on distinct boulders, varied in shape and orientation. A few taller formations loom in the background, presenting more committing lines for those drawn to height and exposure.
