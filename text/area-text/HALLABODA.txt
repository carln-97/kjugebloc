Hallaboda is a varied area both when it comes to terrain and rock-types. Thus far three boulders have been discovered: the smooth <b>Bleaustenen</b> located atop an open hill with a nice view and usually pleasant breeze. <b>Ballerina</b> is a bloc of slightly rougher quality, though with some uniquely rounded formations, elevated on a small hill in open forest. Lastly, there's <b>Papillon</b> a large stone of rather different compact rock-quality, hosting both vertical lines as well as some overhanging challenges. This bloc is located in a depression in the landscape surrounded by dense forest, making it dry slowly.

Landings are all good with some exceptions in the Papillon-sector. The approaches goes through the terrain without much of a path, though it's not very far.

!!<s>ACCESS:</s> if the gate into the area is locked (sometimes it's closed without being locked), park by the gate and walk an additional 500m-1km. Parkings spots are limited and small – park so that large vehicles can pass by.!!
