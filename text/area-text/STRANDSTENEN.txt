As the name suggests, Strandstenen sits right by the water. The bloc offers a number of enjoyable lines, including some on the lake-facing sides that are best climbed in swimwear! The problems are varied and often quite cryptic, with hidden holds and sequences that aren’t immediately obvious. This makes many of the climbs hard to flash and often requires some puzzling before the optimal method is found.

One such climb is "Afterate" — a tricky face problem that, somewhat annoyingly, looks deceptively easy. But topping it out on your first try, without beta, is no small feat! The boulder is very accessible, just a few steps from the parking lot. While rarely visited by climbers, on warmer days it’s often lively with visitors enjoying the lake.

!!<s>ACCESS:</s> no camping in the parking lot.!!
