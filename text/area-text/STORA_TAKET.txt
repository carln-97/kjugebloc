Deep in the forests of northern Skåne lies <b>Stora Taket</b> or the Big Roof. As the name suggests, it’s a roof of considerable proportions (by Skåne standards — temper your expectations). The main area features two large boulders, both offering some of the finest rock quality found in southern Sweden.

The first is the roof itself, home to a range of steep lines. Among them, "Rufus" stands out as quite unique — a steep easier problem that climbs great! The second bloc rises like a monolith, with a proud line running straight up an apparently featureless face. The problem, "Wallstreet", demands a blend of finger strength and cryptic sequences to unlock. A smaller sector, <b>Den Ovisses Sten</b>, consists of three rougher blocks, each with a few nice lines of their own.
