Oppmannaberget is perfect for escaping the crowds and enjoying calm climbing in the gentle slopes of Skåne's dense beech forests. The climbing varies by sector: choose between the tall pillars of Kjuge-esque rock in the western sectors or the few select power climbs on the bullet rock of the <b>Bullseye-sector</b> in the eastern parts. Psyched climbers will certainly find plenty of potential for new lines, though primarily in the easter sectors. Accessing most sectors require a bit of hiking on narrow meandering paths.
