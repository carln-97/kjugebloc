Kallsjön was first discovered in 2003 by a group of local climbers, but remained a secret for thirteen years before word began to spread and others started to recognize the area’s potential. The boulders are scattered spaciously throughout deciduous forest and vary in size. All of the stones are of good quality, but one clearly stands out as the most impressive and demands your full attention!

This is the bloc of "Pippi Långstrump" — without a doubt one of Skåne’s finest lines, featuring powerful, sustained moves on small positive holds up a steep overhanging face! The same boulder also hosts a range of excellent easier lines, each one unique — from slabs to steep sequences on good holds, to mentally demanding mantles. What they all share is the bloc’s impressive height and perfect rock-quality. The remaining boulders also offer many enjoyable problems, even if they can’t quite compete with the main attraction. Most landings are good, and the approach is nothing to worry about.
