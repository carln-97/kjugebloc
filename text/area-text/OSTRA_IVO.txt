Östra Ivö is a steep rocky hillside on the island of Ivö in the lake Ivösjön. The climbing here is wild and spread out, with varied rock types and problems in many different styles. Approaches are long, tough, and sometimes confusing, with uneven terrain and landings throughout.

This area won’t appeal to everyone — but for those interested in trading convenience and volume for solitude and quality, the reward is stoic and inspiring lines deep in a quiet forest. Here in particular, it pays to look for lines over numbers...

!!NATURE-RESERVE: all boulders are located within a nature-reserve — please respect and follow the restrictions. Do not damage trees, plants or the surrounding terrain. Dogs must be on a leash. No camping for longer than 24h.!!
