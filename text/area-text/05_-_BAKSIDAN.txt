The so-called backside of Kjugekull is densely packed with large boulders, with narrow paths winding through tight clusters. While many blocs lie close to the trails, access can be tricky due to the uneven terrain. Most problems are of moderate height, though several striking highballs of varying difficulty stand out. Landings are often slightly uneven, so it’s wise to bring an extra pad—and perhaps a spotter. Sheltered from the wind, Baksidan is ideal on cold winter days. However, this also means it dries slowly after rain.

Despite its secluded feel, Baksidan remains one of the forest’s most popular zones, thanks to its wealth of high-quality lines that continue to attract climbers year after year.
