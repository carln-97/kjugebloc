Arkelstorp is a larger area with many separate parkings, and lots of varying lines. The stone is generally on the rougher side, but with many cool and unusual formations, like the imposing "Transcendent Rytmik" or the spectacularly dynamic "Big G". This area offers a bit of everything: roadside climbs and hidden gems with taxing approaches, highballs together with lowballs, climbing in dense fir forests under a floor of green moss as well as more open surroundings. As mentioned above the approaches differ a lot in length, but are usually straight forward and not so bad. The landings are also good in almost all cases.
