Insidan stretches through open pine forests and across smooth, glacially sculpted ridges. The boulders vary in size, many quite large, and feature the vertical, technical climbing for which Kjugekull is well known. Unlike in many other sectors, however, Insidan also offers steeper lines and overhangs, adding a dose of powerful, dynamic climbing to the mix. The easier problems in this area shouldn’t be underestimated either. A standout example is the distinctive wall <b>Uppvärmningen</b> – true to its name, it hosts a range of excellent slab problems that follow elegant and rounded features, perfect for warming up or for your first outdoor climb!

Several hiking trails weave through the area, making all parts easily accessible. On sunny days, this sector can draw quite a crowd, with climbers lining up to take on classics like "Ahab", "Ferdinand" and "Lithium". Yet for those seeking solitude, there’s always a quiet spot to be found – a hidden gem tucked into the many nooks and crannies of this weaving landscape!
