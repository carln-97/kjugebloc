The first bloc at Värestorp is moderately tall with perfectly flat landings. The stone is compact yet fractured in just the right way, providing many interesting holds. A number of lines goes on all sides of the boulder, and while each problem presents a worthy challenge, "The One That Got Away" stands just a notch above the rest.

The name is a nod to the persistent stone hunter John Burgess, who discovered many of the boulders around Ivösjön. This one, however, slipped past him — but was luckily uncovered by a certain Ehmann instead... It remains one of the few finds that didn’t belong to John.

<b>Ehmanns vägg</b> is a tall and very long wall, though only a shorter section is climbable, the rest leads straight into a swamp. At the time of writing, there’s only a handful of lines: a tall face climb and a few splendid difficult slabs. The wall holds clear potential for several more powerful problems.

!!<s>ACCESS:</s> the parking for the sector <s>'Första blocket'</s> lies next to some houses. The approach to the sector <s>'Ehmanns vägg'</s> goes closely past a private home. Please show extra discretion in both places.!!
