Ängen covers a large, open landscape with smooth fields and some patches of forest, as well as plenty
of boulders! This sector is easily accessible, and many of the problems are less than vertical with soft grassy landings, making it an ideal introduction for newcomers and children.

In the northwestern corner, near Gamla Skogen, stands the imposing <b>Kluvna Stenen</b> — a massive, split boulder that breaks the otherwise approachable character of the sector. Its steep, overhanging lines are bold and challenging, but those who dare are rewarded with fantastic climbing and panoramic views from the top.

Up the hill lies <b>Solberget</b>, a spot especially popular with families. It offers gentle, beginner-friendly problems, scenic surroundings, and a beautiful view over Ivösjön. On warm spring days, this part of Ängen becomes one of the more frequented areas in Kjugekull with its relaxed atmosphere and climbing.
