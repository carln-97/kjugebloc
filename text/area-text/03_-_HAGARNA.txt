Hagarna spans both sides of a small ridge, with boulders scattered across varied terrain. On the west side, closest to the café, are the well-visited spots of Sonic and Första Stenen — as the name suggests the very place where climbing in Kjugekull began! A short hike brings you to Tjurstenen on the more secluded eastern side, a much less frequented part of the woods with smaller rocks and less chalk.

The boulders here are generally of moderate size with comfortable landings, providing a wide range of styles. In the Sonic area, you’ll find some of the hardest lines in the forest — sit starts to climbs like Sonic and Huggsexa stand out not just for their difficulty, but for their quality, ranking among the very best in all of Kjugekull.
