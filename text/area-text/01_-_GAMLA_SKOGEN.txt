Gamla Skogen is one of the oldest parts of Kjugekull, with climbing activity dating back to 1992 — and probably earlier. The spacious beech forest is scattered with boulders of all sizes, from smaller lowballs to massive stone giants tucked into the northernmost reaches.

The landings are generally good and level, though a few of the taller lines call for extra pads. Vertical problems on thin edges and subtle cracks dominate the style, demanding finger strength and precise movement. But you’ll also find technical slabs and the occasional steep overhang.

Well-established trails weave through the area, and the rock dries quickly after rain, making it a popular and dependable spot. Thanks to the variety in height and difficulty, Gamla Skogen welcomes both experienced climbers and beginners alike.
