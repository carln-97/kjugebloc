Outside the nature-reserve, tucked away between apple orchards and cattle fields in the southernmost reaches, lies the small and remote sector of Avkroken. A handful of boulders are scattered throughout the sparse forest, giving it a very quiet and understated feel.

At first glance, it may not seem like much, but looks can be deceiving. For those who take the time to venture out to these often forgotten outskirts, Avkroken offers a few surprisingly high-quality problems. Chief among them is the clean-cut arete of "Splinter", a line that alone makes the longer approach worthwhile.
